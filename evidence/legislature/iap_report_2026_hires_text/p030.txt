The point also being made here is that electoral mapping is an iterative process in which even electoral
boundary commissions have second thoughts about their own recommendations. Likewise, there is nothing
wrong or irregular with the Legislature directing the advisory panel to review the Province's electoral divisions
with a view to providing a "second opinion" or "second thoughts", so to speak.
While the advisory panel is unanimously inclined to recommend a Leduc-Beaumont electoral division, the
problem of its significantly higher than average population remains. And so, to reduce this high population
the independent advisory panel recommends including Nisku, the Edmonton International Airport and areas
around it into a new south Edmonton electoral division we propose to be named "Edmonton-Nisku". The
recommended Edmonton-Nisku electoral division is not an additional electoral division. It would simply
replace the loss of the Edmonton-Beaumont electoral division.
Edmonton-Nisku would consist of Edmonton-South East electoral division, Nisku and areas north of Airport
Road east of Highway 2 and west of the Leduc-Beaumont electoral division. The creation of a new electoral
division also led the advisory panel to consider the restoration of Edmonton-Strathcona, Edmonton-Whitemud,
Edmonton-South West (also known as Edmonton-Windermere), Edmonton-South, Edmonton-Meadows,
Edmonton-Mill Woods and Edmonton-Ellerslie to configurations similar to that which currently exist but
subject to the changes which the 2025-2026 Electoral Boundaries Commission recommended in its Interim
Report. The result of that reconsideration was to recommend restoring these electoral divisions to those
configurations. We deal with those electoral divisions individually later in this report.
