Edmonton-Nisku

Having recommended the restoration of a Leduc-Beaumont electoral division because of the support shown
for it, the advisory panel had to deal with the problem of the electoral division having a significantly higher
than average population. The panel dealt with that problem by moving some of the areas in and around
Leduc and Beaumont into a new south Edmonton electoral division named Edmonton-Nisku.
To prevent the population of Leduc-Beaumont being unreasonably high, the panel recommends including
Nisku Industrial Park and surrounding areas in this electoral division. For example, we are also including
Edmonton Internation Airport and its surrounding areas. These areas are obviously integrated into south
Edmonton's economy, and residents are highly likely to receive services in southeast Edmonton. Including
them in an Edmonton-Nisku electoral division is accordingly reasonable. The electoral division's shape may
look odd. But it is justified given the clear community of interest between Nisku and southeast Edmonton,
and the desirability of the borders of neighbouring electoral divisions.
Edmonton-Nisku is a new electoral division with territory taken from Leduc-Beaumont, but also from
Edmonton-Ellerslie, Edmonton-South and Edmonton Meadows. The electoral division which the panel
recommends is located roughly where Edmonton-South East was recommended to be located in the 2025-
2026 Electoral Boundaries Commission's Interim Report. It includes Nisku Industrial Park and surrounding
areas. It also includes areas north of Beaumont straddling 50th Street, the main road connection between
Beaumont and Edmonton. Its northern boundary is roughly the Anthony Henday Drive although its electoral
boundary does cross over the Anthony Henday Drive to take in a bit of Edmonton-Ellerslie and Edmonton-
Meadows. Its eastern boundary is Edmonton's eastern city limits.
Most of the new Edmonton-Nisku electoral division is within the City of Edmonton, although, of course, it
also takes in northwestern portions of Leduc County. The Edmonton portions are:

   1) Areas in the City of Edmonton, south of 41st Avenue SW, east of Gateway Boulevard, and west of the
   City of Edmonton's eastern limits (taken from Edmonton-Ellerslie and Edmonton-South);
   2) Areas north of 41st Avenue SW, east of 66th Street SW, south of Anthony Henday Drive, and west of the
   city's eastern limits (taken from Edmonton-Ellerslie);
   3) Areas north of Anthony Henday Drive, east of 34th Street NW, south of 16A or 15th Avenue NW and west
   of 17th Street NW (taken from Edmonton-Ellerslie and Edmonton Meadows); and
   4) Areas north of Anthony Henday Drive, east of 50th Street NW, south of 23rd Avenue NW, and west of
   34th Street NW (taken from Edmonton-Ellerslie).
We have no concerns with the boundaries of this electoral division extending beyond the City limits because
it clearly unites communities of interest in southeast Edmonton.
