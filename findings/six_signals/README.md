**Reading.** Putting part of a mid-size city in a mostly rural division is not unique to Lethbridge: the commission's own maps do it for Medicine Hat and Grande Prairie. The report does not apply the same reasoning to Red Deer or Medicine Hat. That is recorded, not explained.

## E. Growth to 2031 (trend, not a projection)

Stated 2024 populations grown at each area's 2021-2025 municipal growth rate.

| Map | Within ±10% (stated, 2024) | Within ±10% (2031 trend) | Beyond ±25% in 2031, not s.15(2) divisions | Correlation of growth with NDP share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Majority | 79 of 89 | 35 | 1 (Peace River −31.7) | 0.71 |
| Minority | 60 of 89 | 22 | 11 (5 Calgary divisions above +25%) | 0.70 |
| Panel | 67 of 91 | 23 | 7 (Calgary-McKenzie +25.9; six rural divisions below −25%) | 0.71 |

On the panel's map, two s.15(2) divisions fall below the −50% floor by 2031 on this trend: Central Peace-Notley −56.6% and Lesser Slave Lake −53.6%. The majority's do too.

**On every map, the fast-growing divisions lean NDP** (correlation about 0.7), because the cities and their suburbs are growing fastest. Over the map's life, divisions of NDP-leaning voters gain people faster than rural ones, so their voters lose relative weight. This holds on all three maps, and the panel's is between the commission maps. Within Calgary and Edmonton every division gets the same city rate, so this measure cannot show differences inside a city.

## F. A neutral redraw of the south only

Running: four chains of 50,000 steps on the 462 voting areas of the panel's southern group. Results will be added here.

**Deviation from the plan, made before any chain result was seen:** the plan said to start each chain from the panel's assignment, and widen the band if needed to admit it. On the voting-area graph two of the panel's districts are not contiguous, and repairing that pushed one district to +62%. So each chain starts from a random plan within the ±25% band, and the first 1,000 steps are discarded. One 6-area piece of Banff-Canmore-Diamond Valley touches the region only through outside areas, so it is held with the nearest region area.
