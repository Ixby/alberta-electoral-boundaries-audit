# The panel's report, checked: graded claims

This file grades each checkable factual claim in the 2026 Independent Advisory Panel (IAP) report on Alberta electoral boundaries, using the inventory in `claims_inventory.md`. It is a fact check. It gives no verdict on whether any map is a gerrymander, and none on whether any proposal is constitutional. Opinions and legal arguments are weighed in the review itself.

**Verdict scale.** *holds* (the source supports the claim as worded) · *partly holds* (some of it is supported; the table says which part) · *does not hold* (the source contradicts the claim) · *cannot be checked from public sources* (the source is unavailable, or the claim's own base cannot be rebuilt).

**Page numbers.** The IAP's own pages are cited as printed pages. PDF page = printed page + 6. The IAP report is SHA-256 `d8ae78abcec3660f8158a327a25df7508b649ce5595788e8b265b362b2a75122`.

**Sources.** Web sources are archived under `evidence/web/` and law sources under `evidence/law/`, each with its SHA-256 in that folder's `MANIFEST.tsv` (filename, URL, retrieval time, hash). Not every listed file is in the repository: third-party pages are recorded by URL and hash only (`evidence/law/README.md`), and so are the BC and Nova Scotia government releases cited in A1 (`bc_news_2015JAG0260.html`, `ns_news_2019-10-09_bill187.html`). The Source column gives the archived filename and the first 12 hex characters of its SHA-256, or a repository path. "Check" files are the working notes each verdict traces to: R = repository (`R_repo`, `checks_repo_arithmetic.md`), W = web, archive and law (`W_web`, `W_archive`, `W_law`). Archive counts are in `archive_counts.md` (same folder), copied from `W_archive`. `R_repo`, `W_web` and `W_law` are not in the repository, and neither are the archive-count scripts, so rows that cite them rest on the sources and figures given in the row. Where the lead reviewer overrode a check file, the row says "(lead review)".

## Method notes

**Source handling.** Web and law sources were fetched on 2 November 2026 and hashed on retrieval (`MANIFEST.tsv` in each evidence folder). Pages that blocked automated access (CanLII, Cloudflare-protected sites, X) were not defeated and are marked as not archived; a verdict that rests on a page read but not archived says so. Wikipedia is used only for secondary context and is flagged.

**Archive counts (M2, N2-N6).** Full method, counts and ID lists are in `archive_counts.md`. In brief:
- *Corpus.* 1,252 written submissions (170 round 1, 1,082 round 2), searched as text, plus hearing transcripts grepped for context. Written texts carry no municipality field, so residence counts rely on the writer's own words and undercount.
- *Search terms.* M2: `beaumont`, `Leduc`, `mayor`. N2: `lethbridge` (150 files). N3: `bearspaw` (8 files) plus residency phrases. N4: `springbank` (25 files) plus split/divide terms. N5: `diamond valley|black diamond|turner valley` co-occurring within 250 characters with Banff, Canmore, Kananaskis or Bow Valley.
- *Labelling rule.* Regexes screened candidates; every file was then read and labelled by hand. N2: PRESERVE = asks to keep Lethbridge as urban-only divisions; HYBRID = asks for rural-urban Lethbridge divisions; NEUTRAL = mentions Lethbridge without a position. M2: a "Beaumont-voice objection" names Beaumont, objects to splitting it or pairing it with Strathcona-Sherwood Park, and speaks from the Beaumont side.
- *Borderline IDs.* Named in `archive_counts.md`: N2 (2-0014, 2-0729, 2-0870, 2-0525, 2-0434, 2-0472, 2-0485, 1-0121, 1-0135, 1-0125, 1-0113, 2-0159, 2-0417); N4 (2-0866, 2-0383); N5 (2-0619 and its hearing presentation); N6 northeast Calgary (2-0842, 2-0933).
- *Not tallied.* Hearing transcripts were not counted for N2. The existing LLM sentiment labels in the repository cover other configurations and were not reused.
- *Unreadable items.* About 34 image-only or empty items (round 1: 1-0010, 1-0012, 1-0014 and others listed under N3 in `archive_counts.md`; round 2: 2-0983, 2-0001, 2-0119) could hold a view that no count here sees.

**Repository geometry and arithmetic (M4-M9, N8-N10, N17).** Geometry was computed in EPSG:3400. Population was the 2021 census dissemination areas apportioned by area overlay, so counts under about 200 people are noise and reserve populations are undercounted (21 DAs carry no population). Commission populations come from the final report's majority list; the panel's from its App. 7. The M6 hybrid recount used a 5,000-resident floor in the city and 500 outside it.

**Law (M17, N16, A1-A4).** SCC decisions were read on the Court's own site (Lexum). CanLII was blocked, so the two ABCA decisions were not read. Where only a news item, a headnote or an information sheet was available, the row says so.

## Not checked / gaps

- **M1, N17:** need the Commission's Interim Report (October 2025) populations and text; it is not in the repository.
- **1991 ABCA 317 and 1994 ABCA 342:** both exist (cited by Alberta commission reports), but the texts were not retrieved. The IAP's quoted wording from both is unverified.
- **Hearing transcripts** were not tallied for N2; a count of oral presenters could differ from the written count.
- **N14:** the Premier's Chief of Staff on X could not be found; the MLA's caucus statement was confirmed at its opening and close only.
- **N16:** full texts of Hunt 2026 FCA 88, Federation acadienne 2024 NSSC 339 and 2017 NSCA 10 (only the information sheet) were not retrieved. The Judges Reference (PEI) and Oakes were not re-read. General-law cites (Vavilov, Baker, Knight and others) were not checked.
- **A1 and A2:** the majority's claims about Yukon, the Northwest Territories and PEI legislation were not independently checked. New Brunswick and Manitoba adoption steps, Newfoundland, PEI and the territories were not examined, and an absence claim cannot be proven.
- **A5:** the panel's boundary file is not in the repository, so (b) and the exclusion of Olds rest on its text and map.
- **M11:** which provincial division contains the Meta site is not stated by any source.
- **M13:** the Choose Lethbridge page and Country Guide were read but are not archived (blocked).
- **Internal consistency:** the 30 discrepancies in section 8 of `analysis/review/iap_report_reading_2026-11.md` are checked in the text layer there and are not re-graded in this file.
- **No verdict is given** on whether any map is a gerrymander, or on whether any proposal is constitutional. Rows A3 and A4 grade only the support the authors cite.
