## §5 applied

**The concession (all five core conditions): not met.** (1) S1 = 0 triggered: met. (2) S2 = 0 triggered: not met (Lethbridge in 4 EDs). (3) S6 = PASS: met. (4) P1, P2 and P3 all PASS: not met (P1 and P3 fail). (5) X2 at most 1 contradicted rationale: met (1, borderline). Conditions 2 and 4 fail on released material alone, so the outcome does not depend on the provisional reading in S1(c) or the borderline one in X2. The concession statement is therefore not published. Of the additional conditions, S5 is blocked and S4 is not scored; X1 is 3 of 4.

**The "sure-sign" rule (four or more of S1, S2, S3 triggered, S4, S5, S6 failed, together with P1–P3 all failed): not met.** One strong signal fires (S2). Even if S4 failed, the count would be 2. P2 passes, so P1–P3 are not all failed either.

**As registered, the intermediate result is reported literally:** one strong signal (a third city, Lethbridge, in four divisions), one weak signal (two rural seats added), two process signals (no hearings, no draft for comment), Recommendation 5 met on three of four conditions, and one borderline rationale contradiction. The checklist does not convert this into a verdict, and nothing here says whether the map is a gerrymander.

**Independent check.** Before this file was pushed, a separate agent re-derived every outcome from the registered text and the sources without seeing this file, then compared. It agreed on 13 of 14 tests. On X2 it rejected the Red Deer item, which was then dropped as above, and judged Morinville-Sturgeon borderline; it also asked for S1(c) to be marked provisional. Both changes are made here.

**Not blind.** This scoring was done after the panel's report, the reconstruction and its provisional results (`findings/a10_stepc/`, `findings/iap_r5_degree/`, `findings/hybrid_regions/`) had been seen. The thresholds and rules are the registered ones, applied as written; where the registered text left a reading open, the reading is stated in the row.
