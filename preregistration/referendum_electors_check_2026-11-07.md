# Referendum electors as a check on the population figures (written before any comparison)

> Written 2026-11-07, after downloading the files and reading their format, before any elector figure has been placed on any map. Unregistered and not blind: the author knows the stated populations and the 2021 overlay figures. Requested by the author on 2026-11-07. GitHub only; no OSF filing. Nothing here is a finding on whether any map is lawful or a gerrymander.

## The population source

The commission and the panel both state their source: the Alberta Office of Statistics and Information's July 1, 2024 estimates at dissemination-area level, total 4,888,723 (commission final report, PDF pp. 28-29; panel report, PDF pp. 107-108). The 2026 census has not reported. **The stated tables built on that source are the primary population figures for the audit's Carter and EBCA scorecard.** The 2021 census overlay is older and is kept only as a labelled side reference.

## The question

Do 2026 elector counts agree better with the stated 2024 estimates or with the 2021 overlay? Electors are not population: they leave out children and non-citizens and depend on registration. The commission rejected electors as a basis for parity under the Act (PDF p. 30). So this is a check on the figures, never a replacement test.

## Data

Elections Alberta official poll results, 2026 referendum, Question 10 workbooks for all 87 divisions (`Number of Electors`, election-day polls) and the division index. Voting-area polygons: `evidence/elections_alberta/EA_Voting_Area_Boundaries_2026.zip`.

**Match check, reported first.** Join on division name and voting-area number. Report the share of polls and electors matched, and whether the poll totals equal the division totals and 3,286,778. If under 99% of electors match, stop and report that the comparison is unreliable.

## What is computed

Electors go to divisions by voting-area representative point, on the majority, minority, panel (v3) and ¶167 maps.

1. **Which figure tracks electors?** On the majority map, whose polygons and stated populations are both official: the correlation of electors with stated population and with the 2021 overlay, across all 89 divisions and across the 49 Calgary and Edmonton divisions. Then the same on the panel and minority maps.
2. **Named cases.** Electors per stated person, and per 2021 overlay person, in Calgary-North East, Calgary-South East and Edmonton-Windermere, against the provincial ratio.
3. **Beside the stated spread.** On each map: elector deviation from the average, the count beyond ±25%, the count within ±10%, and the Calgary and Edmonton mean against the rest.
4. **Unusual divisions.** Divisions whose electors per stated person are furthest from the provincial ratio, listed by name.

## How the result is read

- If the stated figures track electors at least as well as the overlay overall and within the two cities, the overlay is not treated as evidence against s.15(1), and it stays only as a side note.
- If the overlay tracks electors better, that is reported as an open question about the estimates. It would not by itself change a s.15(1) grade, because the Act's test is population.
- A division beyond ±25% on electors is not beyond ±25% under the Act, and is reported only as a description.
- Whether the estimates satisfy s.12 stays unresolved: the commission and panel give their reasons, and no court has ruled on this use.
