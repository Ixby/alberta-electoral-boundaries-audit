Edmonton-South

Edmonton-South is an electoral division west of Highway 2 (Calgary Trail or Gateway Boulevard). It runs
from the City of Edmonton's southern limits north to Ellerslie Road. Its western boundary follows Whitemud
Creek for most of its length. Edmonton-South is a rapidly growing electoral division. Subject to the changes
described below, the electoral division that the panel recommends is very similar to that which currently
exists and has existed since at least 2017.
The elimination of Edmonton-Beaumont and the creation of Edmonton-Nisku enabled the current
boundaries of this electoral division to be restored subject to the following changes:

   1. It is proposed that this electoral division lose territory along its northern boundary, given its growth in
      population. All areas north of Ellerslie Road and west of Gateway Boulevard are recommended to be
      moved to Edmonton-Rutherford.

   2. Areas north of Anthony Henday Drive and west of Whitemud Creek are proposed to be moved to
      Edmonton-Whitemud. This will help balance the populations in the electoral divisions and create
      clearer boundaries.

   3. In addition, it is proposed that the community of Chappelle Gardens be moved to Edmonton-South
      West (which the 2025-2026 Electoral Boundaries Commission renamed Edmonton-Windermere) to
      reduce the population of Edmonton-South.

   4. And, finally, it is recommended that areas east of Gateway Boulevard be moved into Edmonton-
      Ellerslie, both because Gateway Boulevard is a more logical location for the boundary, and also to
      reduce the population of Edmonton-South.

We are now able to recommend an electoral division with clearer and more understandable boundaries.
This electoral division brings together several neighbourhoods in south Edmonton with clearer boundaries.
The population remains high, as does its growth potential, but it is much lower than under the current
boundaries.
