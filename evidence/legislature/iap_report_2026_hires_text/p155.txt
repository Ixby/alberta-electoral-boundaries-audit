VII) Other Concerns

A. The Partisan Implications and Circumstances of the Calgary and Lethbridge Changes Must
     Be Considered

144. We would be remiss not to note that our colleagues' proposed changes to the maps of Calgary and
     Lethbridge have obvious partisan implications. As Chief Justice Roberts observed for the Supreme Court
     of the United States in Rucho v Common Cause, 588 U.S. 684, 692-93 (2019), such partisan implications do
     not, without more, dispose of questions of propriety. All changes may have partisan implications. But in
     the circumstances of this Panel, we must be particularly sensitive to making changes that have obvious
     partisan implications and are not obviously necessary for effective representation. We say this for two
     reasons.
