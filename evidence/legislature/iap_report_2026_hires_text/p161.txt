164. Canada's democracy has endured by placing the populace, rather than their representatives, at the centre
     of this process. There is no perfect solution because every decision involves trade-offs. We are confident
     that the map of 91 electoral divisions that our Minority Report proposes is a reasonable balance of those
     trade-offs.

165. The rare instances where electoral boundaries commissions' decisions have not been followed have, as we
     noted above, usually been accepted by all political parties. These are norms working well: incredibly
     interesting to politics nerds, but generally boring.

166. In an unhealthy democracy where norms are not followed, however, the heavy hand of the law may have
     no choice but to intervene. This 2025-2026 Alberta redistricting process has pushed the envelope of
     democratic norms. Acting beyond the restoration of the two rural electoral divisions, and adopting our
     colleagues' proposed maps south of Airdrie, will likely force the courts to enter this fray.
