Minority Opinion - Panel of the Special Select Committee on Electoral Boundaries  28

106. Even if we were to accept that effective representation requires Springbank to be in a Calgary electoral
     division, we find dividing it between Calgary-West and Calgary-Bow to be odd. This is the only way that
     the IAP Majority has been able to solve the population problem of adding Springbank to Calgary. This
     population problem was another reason for the ABEBC to treat Springbank differently than Elbow Valley.
     Dividing Springbank was never proposed to the ABEBC. We also note that Calgary-Bow extends as far
     east as the community of Bowness--which has a radically different culture and economic profile than
     Springbank.

107. Including Bearspaw Village in Calgary-North West is the most defensible change proposed by our
     colleagues. It has a certain logic to it given it is otherwise sharing an electoral division with Cochrane that
     is experiencing high population growth (which may attenuate our concerns about population) and the
     closeness of Bearspaw Village to Calgary. However, Bearspaw Village, though urban to some extent, is a
     distinct residential community with acreage infrastructure and independent utility systems. While it is
     adjacent to Calgary and closer to Calgary than Cochrane, there are aspects of life in Bearspaw Village that
     are more similar to Cochrane, as Ms. Brown observed.

108. Accordingly, it is our view that reasonable people can disagree about where these communities belong.
     Systematically increasing the populations of Calgary electoral divisions to place these areas in Calgary
     electoral divisions is accordingly not necessary to ensure effective representation. This also creates
     constitutional vulnerability given Justice McLachlin's holdings in Carter that population deviations can only
     be justified if they cause more effective representation or better governance. Returning to this Panel's
     mandate, under no reasonable definition of "necessary" can it be said to be "necessary" to include these
     communities in Calgary electoral divisions.

109. Alberta Court of Appeal precedent further supports the view that, whatever the merits for including
     Springbank and Bearspaw in Calgary in a map of 89 electoral divisions, this is weakened in a map of 91.
     Had these communities been included in Calgary electoral divisions in ABEBC Majority Report map of
     89 electoral divisions, we could in turn see the non-city portions of Airdrie-East moving to other electoral
     divisions. This would have enabled a purely Airdrie seat (Airdrie-East), as well as a maintained, if oddly
     shaped, Airdrie-Cochrane. Accordingly, the use of hybrids would have eliminated the need for an Airdrie-
     West. This, in turn, would have saved one of the two rural electoral divisions that the ABEBC Majority
     Report removed. This clearly would not have been what the City of Airdrie wanted, however. And the
     populations of both Airdrie-East and Airdrie-Cochrane electoral divisions would have remained
     undesirably high given the growth in them. Nonetheless, this would have accorded with endorsement
     from the Court of Appeal that hybrid electoral divisions are constitutionally permissible to avoid the
     elimination of rural seats: Reference re: Order in Council 215/93 Respecting the Electoral Divisions
     Statutes Amendment Act, 1994 ABCA 342 ("1994 Reference") at paras 71-74. However, we are not the first
     instance decision-maker and it is not our role to relitigate the ABEBC's holdings.

110. Moreover, the hypothetical in the preceding paragraph demonstrates how one rural electoral division could
     have been saved through the creative use of hybrid electoral divisions around Calgary. But--and this is
     the crucial point--one rural electoral division would still have been removed. The legislature has achieved
     its objective of saving rural seats entirely through increasing the legislature's size: increasing the
     population of Calgary electoral divisions through more hybrids is thus unwarranted.
