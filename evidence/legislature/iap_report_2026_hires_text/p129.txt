portions of Clearwater County. While the Panel recognizes that others may disagree that this electoral
division can be maintained in a map of only 91 electoral divisions, we nonetheless are of the view that it
does not offend s 3 of the Charter given the unique circumstances in which this Panel arose. We do grant
that it may be more difficult to justify this electoral division under s 1 of the Charter if a court were to
disagree with the Panel concerning s 3: it may take a map of 93 or 95 electoral divisions before Rocky
Mountain House-Sundre's restoration could be considered "minimally impairing" of the right to vote of
urban Albertans. This electoral division's recreation reduces the populations of the electoral divisions in
the Highway 2 corridor considerably. We nonetheless are of the view that s 3 is not limited at all, so we
do not need to consider this issue concerning s 1.

36. While the Panel is of the view that "Rocky Mountain House-Sundre" is a simple and clear name for this
     electoral division, "Clearwater", "David Thompson", "Eastern Slopes", and "Ya Ha Tinda" may better
     evoke the spirit of this electoral division. The Panel is content to defer to the legislature's naming choice.

37. We, the IAP Minority, note that the IAP Majority also recommends including approximately 1,300 people
     in the City of Red Deer in the Blackfalds-Innisfail electoral division. We would not be raising concerns
     about this if it were our only concern with the IAP Majority's maps. However, we find this change to be
     unnecessary and ill-advised. It is challenging enough as a municipality or resident thereof to get an MLA's
     attention when the municipality is the primary part of an electoral division. Getting their attention and
     understanding of all the municipal issues to serve only a handful of residents, when there are already two
     other MLAs that have the primary relationship with this community, seems suboptimal to us.

38. The vast majority of the 91 electoral divisions that this Panel unanimously proposes are either the same as
     the ABEBC Majority Report, or the Interim Report. This preserves both the letter and the spirit of
     Recommendation 5.

V) Defining Our Mandate

39. We now turn to the question of whether this Panel has the mandate to advise on changes to Alberta's
     electoral map unrelated to the restoration of the two rural electoral divisions. It should go without saying
     that understanding our mandate is a crucial part of fulfilling our mandate.
