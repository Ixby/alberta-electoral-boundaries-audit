Seats (UCP–NDP) on each map as the province-wide lead moves. Blue: UCP majority. Orange: NDP majority. Grey: no majority.
How it was run: 338Canada's riding projections of October 19, 2026, built from polls up to October 16 (rounded headline 48–43; 47.6–43.4 once added up riding by riding), applied to each map using where 2023's votes were cast. Top: seats at 338's central numbers; Red Deer-North is an exact tie. "Needs +4.3": the province-wide UCP lead over the NDP needed for a majority. Panel maps are the audit's reconstructions. With polling uncertainty, the UCP wins a majority in 54–62% of simulations on the other four maps and 78% on the commission minority's; across every model variant tried, 42–68% and 76–82%.
