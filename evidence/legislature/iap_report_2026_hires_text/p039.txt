Edmonton-Meadows

Edmonton-Meadows is an electoral division east of Edmonton-Mill Woods and Edmonton-Ellerslie
bordering Edmonton's eastern city limits. The 2025-2026 Electoral Boundaries Commission recommended
that western portions of the current Edmonton-Meadows electoral division west of 34th Street N.W. be
moved into Edmonton-Mill Woods and Edmonton-Ellerslie to create Edmonton-Beaumont. The removal
of Edmonton-Beaumont has not changed that. Since we are adopting the recommendations of the
Commission's Interim Report, it is still recommended that the western portions of the electoral division be
moved into Edmonton-Mill Woods and Edmonton-Ellerslie.
The Edmonton-Meadows electoral division as mapped by the 2025-2026 Electoral Boundaries Commission
in its Interim Report (and in its Final Report) has clear and understandable boundaries. Its northern boundary
is still Whitemud Drive or Highway 14 (the highway to Viking and Wainwright) and 38th Avenue N.W. Its
southern boundary is still Anthony Henday Drive and 16A Avenue N.W. Its western boundary is still 34th
Street N.W. and its eastern boundary is Edmonton's city limits or a north-south portion of the Anthony
Henday Drive.
Rapid growth is anticipated in this area and so its smaller than average population is justified for the
Edmonton-Meadows electoral division. Its smaller geographic size facilitates effective representation.
