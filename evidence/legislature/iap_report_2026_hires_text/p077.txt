Cochrane

In 2017, the 2016-2017 Electoral Boundaries Commission recommended taking Cochrane out of a Banff-
Cochrane electoral division and putting it in an electoral division with west Airdrie to give Cochrane a voice
undiluted by the concerns of Alberta's mountain park communities.
The 2025-2026 Electoral Boundaries Commission recommended removing West Airdrie and a portion of
Rocky View County and putting Cochrane into an electoral division with Springbank south of Cochrane.
What the 2025-2026 Electoral Boundaries Commission did made sense, but the advisory panel believes that
Springbank's connection to Calgary is even stronger than its connection to Cochrane. This is, the advisory
panel is of the view that what makes more sense is to put Springbank in electoral divisions in Calgary on
the premise that the connection of most people in Springbank to the people in Calgary is much stronger
than their connection to Cochrane (see elsewhere in this report the explanations for recommending that
Springbank be put in electoral divisions in Calgary).
Taking Springbank out of Cochrane, of course, removed a significant amount of population from the
Cochrane electoral division. However the way the advisory panel has mapped the Cochrane electoral
division, it still has about 48,000 people in it or about 11% below average. And the advisory panel's
recommendation has the benefit of giving Cochrane its own elected representative. Furthermore, Cochrane
is expected to grow rapidly so that any deviation from the average may be short-lived.
