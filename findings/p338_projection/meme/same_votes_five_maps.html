How it was run: 338Canada's riding projections (Oct 19, 2026; rounded headline 48–43, 47.6–43.4 once added up riding by riding) applied to each map, using where 2023's votes were cast. Seats are at 338's central numbers; Red Deer-North is an exact tie. "Needs +4.3": how far ahead of the NDP the UCP must be province-wide to win a majority on that map. Dashed line: majority. Panel maps are the audit's reconstructions. A projection, not a poll: with polling uncertainty the UCP wins a majority in 54–62% of simulations on the first four maps, 78% on the commission minority's.
