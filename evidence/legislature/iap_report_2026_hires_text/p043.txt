Edmonton-Whitemud

Three changes to this electoral division as recommended by the 2025-2026 Electoral Boundaries
Commission are also recommended by the advisory panel.
The first is to add an area east of Rabbit Hill Road north of the Anthony Henday Drive between Rabbit Hill
Road and Whitemud Creek. This was recommended by the 2025-2026 Electoral Boundaries Commission in
its Interim Report and Final Report.
Secondly, instead of the eastern boundary following Whitemud Drive north to the North Saskatchewan
River, the advisory panel recommends it follow Whitemud Creek north to the River. Areas west of Whitemud
Creek (between Whitemud Creek and Whitemud Drive) have thereby been added to the electoral division.
Reverting back to the Whitemud Creek boundary increases the population of the electoral division.
Thirdly, all areas south of Anthony Henday Drive are recommended to be removed. This makes a lot of sense.
The Anthony Henday is a major barrier both physically and psychologically.
Adding the area between Rabbit Hill Road and Whitemud Creek makes the Edmonton-Whitemud electoral
division, arguably, a clearer and more understandable electoral division between the Anthony Henday Drive
on the south and the North Saskatchewan River on the north, with Whitemud Creek forming its eastern
boundary and the North Saskatchewan River forming not only its northern boundary but also its western
boundary. The electoral division now has a configuration similar to Edmonton-South West (Edmonton-
Windermere) to the south.
The recommended mapping also addressed a criticism of the current mapping by bringing Magrath Heights
and Mactaggart, two Terwillegar communities, into the electoral division with the rest of Terwillegar.
