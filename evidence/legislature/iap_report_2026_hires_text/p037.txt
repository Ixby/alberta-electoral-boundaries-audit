Edmonton-Ellerslie

The advisory panel's recommendation to restore a Leduc-Beaumont electoral division, as suggested by
the Chair of the 2025-2026 Electoral Boundaries Commission, required the creation of a new electoral
division to replace the Edmonton-Beaumont electoral division, namely Edmonton-Nisku. In order to create
Edmonton-Nisku, all areas of Edmonton-Ellerslie east of 50th Street N.W. and north of the Anthony Henday
Drive are recommended to be moved to Edmonton-Nisku. However, the Edmonton-Ellerslie electoral
division gains areas south of 25th Avenue S.W. and east of Gateway Boulevard from the Edmonton-South
electoral division which previously straddled Gateway Boulevard. That straddling was not considered ideal
and has now been eliminated.
As a consequence, Edmonton-Ellerslie, arguably, becomes a more reasonable and understandable electoral
division on the east side of Highway 2 or Gateway Boulevard, running from the City's south city limit (41st
Avenue S.W.) north to 23rd Avenue N.W. (i.e. north of the Anthony Henday Drive). Its eastern boundary, south
of the Anthony Henday Drive, is 66th Street S. W. Its eastern boundary, north of the Anthony Henday Drive, is
50th Street N.W. while its north boundary is 23rd Avenue N.W.
The electoral division consists of the Mill Woods communities of Satoo, Knottwood, Menisa, Ekota, and
Sakaw north of the Anthony Henday Drive and the neighborhoods of Ellerslie, Summerside and the Orchards
at Ellerslie south of Anthony Henday Drive.
It has clear boundaries and a reasonable, if growing, population.
