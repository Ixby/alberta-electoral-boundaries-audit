6. Two rural electoral divisions can be restored:

     a) Lac Ste. Anne (a successor electoral division to Lac Ste. Anne-Parkland); and
     b) Rocky Mountain House-Sundre (a successor electoral division to Rimbey-Rocky Mountain House-

          Sundre), receiving protection pursuant to s 15(2) of the Act.

     Restoration of these two electoral divisions not only results in effective representation for these areas, but
     also improves effective representation for South Edmonton. The cascading consequences outside of
     Edmonton do not extend to any electoral division containing territory south of Airdrie's southernmost
     border, with the exceptions of Drumheller-Stettler and, to a very modest extent affecting fewer than 1,000
     individuals, Canmore-Banff. This adheres to the strict non-partisan terms that Justice Miller imposed in
     Recommendation 5. Only 14 non-Edmonton1 electoral divisions from the ABEBC Majority Report need
     be affected by the restoration of these two electoral divisions. All fourteen of these electoral divisions
     provide more effective representation than what was proposed in the ABEBC Majority Report. Many of
     them are more similar to the current electoral divisions, which preserves the status quo.

7. We appreciate that our fellow panelists have approached this process in good faith, and are grateful that
     we have unanimously agreed on the maps of the vast majority of the 91 electoral divisions. Our reasoning
     nonetheless differs significantly so we feel compelled to write separately.

8. The majority of this panel has interpreted the Panel's mandate to extend beyond the restoration of the
     two rural electoral divisions. Our colleagues are of the view that the Panel can suggest other changes that
     are "necessary" to facilitate effective representation. We disagree that it is even in the Panel's purview to
     be making recommended revisions to electoral divisions proposed by the ABEBC's Majority Report that
     are unrelated to the restoration of Lac Ste. Anne and Rocky Mountain House-Sundre. However, even if
     the ambiguous Motion 37 is interpreted broadly, there is still a standard of "necessity". In other words,
     the fact that an alternative map would also achieve effective representation in a different way is insufficient
     to permit the Panel to depart from the ABEBC Majority Report. Only if the change is necessary to achieve
     effective representation may the Panel do so. This is a high standard that the Panel must interpret in good
     faith. This process is not a do-over or a chance to correct perceived errors by the ABEBC Majority. Our
     colleagues disagree and significantly rework the Commission Majority's boundaries in Calgary and
     Southern Alberta.

9. Our colleagues propose moving South Springbank to Calgary-West-Elbow Valley, North Springbank to
     Calgary-Bow, and Bearspaw Village to Calgary-North West. To be sure, these maps of Calgary, unlike
     those proposed by the ABEBC Minority Report, would likely provide the constitutional minimum
     assurance of effective representation. However, they still subtly dilute the votes of Calgarians in ways that
     are unnecessary to achieve effective representation. Moreover, the conclusion that they are necessary to
     achieve effective representation is a collateral attack on the ABEBC Majority Report. We have similar
     concerns with respect to possible changes to Calgary-Confluence, Calgary-Cross, Calgary-East, and
     Calgary-Klein.
