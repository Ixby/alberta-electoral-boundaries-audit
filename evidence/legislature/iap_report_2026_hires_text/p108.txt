Having divided the Province into 91 electoral divisions, as the advisory panel was mandated to do, the panel
arrived at an average population of the proposed 91 electoral divisions, employing the following formula:

    Total Estimated Population = The average population of the electoral division
     No. of Electoral Divisions
    4,888,723 = 53,722

         91
As a consequence, the advisory panel based its proposals on an average electoral division population of
53,722.
Section 15(1) of the Electoral Boundaries Commission Act states that the population of a proposed electoral
division must not be more than 25% above nor more than 25% below. Twenty-five percent of 53,722 is 13,430.
Therefore, the population of an electoral division may vary from 40,292 to 67,152. (There is provision in the
Act for four special consideration electoral divisions, but the 25% rule applies unless the statutory criteria for
special consideration electoral divisions are met, and then only in respect of four electoral divisions.)
There have been suggestions that electoral boundaries mapping should be based on the number of electors
in any given electoral division. That would require an amendment to the Electoral Boundaries Commission
Act which states that it is the population, not the number of electors, which must meet the plus or minus
25% rule. That is, it is population, not electors, which an electoral boundaries commission and the advisory
panel must consider in recommending electoral boundaries.
In the last general election only 60% of those registered to vote voted and voter turnout percentages varied
widely from one electoral division to the next. They also vary widely over time. But, regardless, once elected,
an MLA is supposed to or assumed to represent everyone in the electoral division, not just those who voted.
And that is a reason why population is used to determine the areas of electoral divisions. Basing electoral
boundaries on numbers of actual voters, or on the number of people registered to vote, is something which
would have to be studied carefully. It is not unprecedented, but the panel is not in a position to recommend it
or recommend against it.
