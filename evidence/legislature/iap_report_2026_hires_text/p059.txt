Drumheller-Stettler

The restoration of the Rocky Mountain House-Sundre electoral division gave the advisory panel the
opportunity to reconfigure the Highway 2 corridor electoral divisions. As a consequence, a portion of the
Drumheller-Stettler electoral division west of Buffalo Lake, which the 2025-2026 Electoral Boundaries
Commission recommended adding to the electoral division, no longer needs to be added. The portion
recommended to be added would have brought Drumheller-Stettler's northwest boundary right up to
Highway 2 and the eastern borders of Blackfalds, Lacombe and Morningside. The advisory panel is now in a
position to recommend that the northwestern boundary run north-south just east of Stettler through Nevis to
Buffalo Lake.
Drumheller-Stettler is a huge electoral division, with the Red Deer River as its western boundary and the
Saskatchewan border as its eastern boundary. Its northern boundary follows roads to the Saskatchewan
border northeast of Provost. It contains Stettler at its northwest end, Provost at its northeast end, Drumheller
on its west boundary and Empress (near Medicine Hat) in its extreme southeast. It contains Stettler, Castor,
Coronation, Consort, Hanna, Special Areas, Youngstown and Oyen and Forestburg on the Battle River in
the north.
