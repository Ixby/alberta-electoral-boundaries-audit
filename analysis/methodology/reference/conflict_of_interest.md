# Conflict-of-interest disclosure

*(File created 2026-07-13. The academic report's §11 had cited this path as "on
file" since before this date; the disclosure lived only in the report's
cover-page CoI block. This file now exists as the citable standalone record and
mirrors that block. Statements of the author's political history are author
attestations.)*

**Funding.** This research received no external funding. All costs were borne
by the author.

**Political activity.** The author has voted for and donated to both
conservative and progressive provincial parties in Alberta as an adult.

**Relationships.** The author has worked as a contract election officer for
Elections Alberta for about 27 years, election by election, administering the
vote on election day. The author is not under contract now and last worked at
a recent election.
The author has had no other employment with Elections Alberta, has no
knowledge of its work beyond those tasks, and drew nothing in this audit from
that work; every input is public. This research is the author's own, not
Elections Alberta's, and does not speak for it. The author has no employment, contractual,
or advisory relationship with the Electoral Boundaries Commission or any
provincial political party. Neither the NDP nor the UCP has been contacted
about this research at any stage, and neither party has had any involvement in
or advance knowledge of it. No compensation was received from any party.

**Institutional.** The author is a student at Mount Royal University. The
research was conducted independently and was not commissioned as coursework;
the only individuals with substantive pre-publication knowledge of the
findings are volunteer reviewers at MRU, which does not constitute
institutional endorsement. Elections Alberta's GIS team was made aware of the
research through the author's formal shapefile request and provided data
access and technical assistance (2026-05-06 and 2026-05-07); Elections Alberta
has not been shown the audit's findings or conclusions.

**Methodological safeguard.** The methodology was pre-registered and applied
symmetrically to both commission maps before results were examined; random
seeds were committed to the public Cloudflare drand beacon before execution.
Findings that ran against the author's expectation are retained and labelled
in both reports.
