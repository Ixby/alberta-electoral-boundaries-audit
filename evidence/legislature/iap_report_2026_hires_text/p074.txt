All of this simply to indicate that including South Springbank in the Calgary-West electoral division is
worthy of consideration by an advisory panel which has been mandated to review the province's electoral
divisions. It is not, as been insinuated, unconstitutional gerrymandering. Given the Commission Chairman's
comments in his Addendum to the Majority Report and the description of the advisory panel's mandate in
Government Motion 37, it is clearly within the panel's mandate to review and make proposals with respect to
this electoral division.
To suggest, as two of our panel members have, that adding Springbank to Calgary-West and Calgary-Bow
subtly dilutes Calgarian's votes assumes there is no commonality of interest between a resident of Aspen
Gardens and a resident of Springbank. One's vote is only diluted if a large number of people with whom
one doesn't share the same interests is added to his or her electoral division. The argument of the minority
assumes there is no commonality of interest between Calgary-West and Calgary-Bow and Springbank. Yet
the Commission assumed there was a commonality of interest when it put Elbow Valley in the Calgary-West
electoral division. Besides, the number of people proposed to be added to the electoral division is not great
compared to the number of people in the former Calgary-West constituency.
As previously indicated in our review of Calgary-Bow-Springbank, the Springbank area south of the
TransCanada Highway is very much a community which Calgarians visit, recreate in, etc.; and Calgary is very
much a community Springbank relies on for services, employment, education, healthcare, etc.
Support for the assertion that South Springbank is very much a Calgary community can be found in the
fact that there are two 18-hole golf courses, one 27-hole golf course and one 45-hole golf course located in
South Springbank. The 45-hole course is affiliated with an athletic club which is located in the City's core.
Its members go from the downtown club to the Springbank golf course on a daily basis as do hundreds of
others to other golf courses in Springbank. Calaway Park is an amusement park that attracts thousands
of Calgarians into Springbank daily. And because there are almost no commercial services in South
Springbank, residents there must commute into Calgary for their daily needs. And, of course, most residents
are employed at jobs in Calgary.
South Springbank is also the location chosen by the Province for its $850-million Springbank off-stream dry
storage reservoir built to protect communities in the City of Calgary from flooding. And some, but not all,
Springbank residents are on the City's water system. It is evident that shared communities of interest go
both ways.
In conclusion, the advisory panel proposes that the Legislature consider approving the Calgary-West-
Springbank electoral division as the panel has proposed it, rather than put South Springbank in a Cochrane
electoral division.
