Wetaskiwin-Ponoka

This is the most northerly Highway 2 electoral division that the restoration of a Rocky Mountain House
electoral division permitted the advisory panel to rationalize (i.e. propose changes to what the 2025-2026
Electoral Boundaries Commission recommended).
The significant change here is the inclusion of Ponoka in the electoral division. Ponoka is currently in an
electoral division with Lacombe. The 2025-2026 Electoral Boundaries Commission recommended moving
Ponoka into this electoral division. The advisory panel concurs.
The current Maskwacis-Wetaskiwin electoral division that was mapped by the 2017 Electoral Boundaries
Commission, and which was in place for the last general election, is a compact and understandable
electoral division comprised of Wetaskiwin, the four First Nations at Maskwacis, as well as Millet, Pigeon
Lake, Falun and Westerose. The advisory panel tried to mimic the current electoral division as best we could.
However, we had to recommend extending the electoral division west to Alder Flats taking in Battle Lake,
Winfield and Buck Lake. To the southwest, the panel recommends extending the electoral division to take in
Rimbey. Like the Commission, the advisory panel recommends moving the northeast portion of the electoral
division into the Camrose electoral division, including Kavanagh, New Sarepta and Hay Lakes.
