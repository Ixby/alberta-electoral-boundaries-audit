Edmonton-Strathcona

The advisory panel reviewed this electoral division and recommends mapping the Edmonton-Strathcona
electoral division as it was mapped in the Commission's Interim Report. The difference between the
mapping of Edmonton-Strathcona in the Commission's Interim Report and its mapping of the electoral
division in its Final Report was marginal. In the Interim Report, the west boundary of the electoral division
was Whitemud Creek. In the Final Report, it was Whitemud Drive (the freeway). The panel recommends that
the west boundary be Whitemud Creek.
The Edmonton-Strathcona electoral division has been around for at least 50 years in different configurations
and with different names and so the advisory panel is not inclined to recommend anything different.
Other changes the 2025-2026 Electoral Boundaries Commission recommended to the current (2017)
electoral division were to move portions east of 99th Street and north of 63rd Avenue or Argyll Road into
Edmonton Gold Bar (neighborhoods like Ritchie, Hazeldean and Argyll). The Commission also expanded the
electoral division to the west to take in portions of Edmonton Riverview electoral division, (i.e. McKernan,
Parkallen, Belgravia, Windsor Park, Grandview Heights and Lansdowne). The advisory panel concurs with the
foregoing Commission recommendations.
The resultant electoral division makes a lot of sense. Some would argue it is now a University of Alberta
centered electoral division, bounded on the south by Whitemud Drive, on the north by the North
Saskatchewan River, on the west, by Whitemud Creek and on the east by Gateway Boulevard, 99th Street
N.W. and the Mill Creek Ravine.
