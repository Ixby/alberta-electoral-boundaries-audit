3) Small and Mid-Size Cities Too Small to Justify a Single Electoral Division

98. The last group of electoral divisions that are identified as "hybrid" are not truly "hybrid" electoral divisions
     at all. They are simply what takes place in all of the rest of the electoral divisions of cities with populations
     less than 40,292: communities that are geographically proximate and seem to have some shared interests
