II) Brief Summary on the Facts, the Law, and Ability to Respond
        to Our Colleagues

11. Except as indicated below, this minority report relies upon the ABEBC Majority Report for its statements
     regarding the law, the facts, and the summary of the submissions that the ABEBC heard as it conducted
     its work in 2025-2026. We are very familiar with the report, the facts, the law, and the submissions. We
     make further statements regarding the law and the submissions in this Report only to buttress our
     recommendations. This report must be understood, in many ways, as a coda to the ABEBC Majority
     Report. It is not possible to understand our Report without first reading the ABEBC Majority Report. We
     essentially view this Panel's work as a continuation and completion of the ABEBC's work.

12. We have reviewed the Panel Majority's rationales for all proposed changes to electoral divisions from the
     ABEBC majority report. We have further reviewed a draft of the Majority's analysis of this Panel's mandate
     and its Executive Summary. We have been unable to review other parts of the Majority's opinion, as the
     Majority was unable to provide them to us in advance for us to be able to do so. This is not a criticism of
     the Chair, who took on an enormous task when almost no one else was willing to do so. Moreover, as a
     result of our deliberations, we consider ourselves to have a good understanding of the Panel Majority's
     rationale and philosophy.

13. However, we are not absolutely certain what our colleagues' opinions are. We therefore hedge somewhat
     throughout this IAP Minority Report. It is our understanding that the IAP Majority:

a) considers this Panel's mandate to extend beyond restoration of the two rural electoral divisions;
b) supports changes from the ABEBC Majority Report to include the communities of Bearspaw and

     Springbank in West Calgary electoral divisions;
c) supports the Committee considering changes confined to the four Calgary electoral divisions of

     Calgary-Confluence, Calgary-Cross, Calgary-East, and Calgary-Klein; and
d) supports changes to approximately eight electoral divisions in the south and southwest of the

     province, which have cascading impacts as far north as Banff and Wheatland County.

We regret that we have been unable to confirm all of these details. However, we are confident that our
fundamental philosophy and opinion stand even if we are mistaken about some of these details.

14. We also note that the IAP Majority discusses at considerable length how the next redistribution should
     proceed. We disagree strongly with these portions of the IAP Majority Report. But, in the interests of
     being as concise as possible, we do not respond here.
