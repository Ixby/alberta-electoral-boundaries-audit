Olds-Didsbury-Three Hills

This is the electoral division that the 2025-2026 Electoral Boundaries Commission named Mountain View-
Kneehill. The current electoral division, as mapped by the 2017 Electoral Boundaries Commission, was
called Olds-Didsbury-Three Hills in the last general election and in the recent 2025 by-election. The advisory
panel recommends keeping the old name because it is arguably more descriptive of the electoral division.
The 2025-2026 Electoral Boundaries Commission reluctantly concluded that they had to add portions of
Mountain View County that were previously in the deleted Rimbey-Rocky Mountain House-Sundre electoral
division to the newly named Mountain View-Kneehill electoral division. Now that Rocky Mountain House-
Sundre has been substantially restored, extending Olds-Didsbury-Three Hills out to the west of Sundre is no
longer necessary.
The Commission also included the Town of Bowden in this electoral division but conceded that it could just
as easily have been put in Sylvan Lake-Innisfail. Bowden is between Innisfail and Olds on Highway 2. The
Commission's reason for including the Town of Bowden in Mountain View-Kneehill (Olds-Didsbury-Three
Hills) was that it balanced the electoral division's population with Sylvan Lake-Innisfail (now Blackfalds-
Innisfail). We accept that view and concur.
The Commission also moved Crossfield and Irricana out of the Olds-Didsbury-Three Hills electoral division
to create the new Airdrie East electoral division. We concur in that recommendation as well.
The 2025-2026 Electoral Boundaries Commission also recommended moving parts of the extreme
southeast of the Olds-Didsbury-Three Hills electoral division into the High River-Vulcan-Siksika electoral
division they recommended, including Rockyford, Rosebud, Standard and Hussar. The advisory panel
found itself recommending the opposite. The panel is recommending that Rockyford, Rosebud, Standard
and Hussar be included in Olds-Didsbury-Three Hills in order to prevent Little Bow, a new electoral division
recommended by the panel, from becoming too large. This proposal is not unlike what the
2017 Electoral Boundaries Commission proposed to keep Cardston-Siksika from becoming too large. The
panel recommends that the southeast boundary of Olds-Didsbury-Three Hills extend south to the Siksika
First Nations Reserve where it will adjoin the recommended Little Bow electoral division in a manner
similar to the way the current Olds-Didsbury-Three Hills electoral division adjoins Cardston-Siksika. The
recommended Little Bow electoral division is explained later in this report.
