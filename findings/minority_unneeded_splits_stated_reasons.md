# Municipalities the minority map splits without need, and the reasons its report gives

**Written:** 2026-10-31 10:20 MDT (Alberta time).

A split is unneeded when the municipality's people would fit in one district inside the Act's ±25% band, yet a second district holds at least 2% of them (Amendment 17's piece rule). People are 2021 census dissemination areas (DAs) laid over Elections Alberta's official 2026 boundaries. Municipal limits are 2021 census limits. The majority map has no unneeded split by this count.

## How firm each split is

DAs nest inside municipalities, so a DA cut by a district line means the line runs through the town's own territory. Where the line cuts a DA, its people are shared out by area, which assumes they are spread evenly. A split resting only on cut DAs could be empty land on the town's edge.

| Municipality | People in the second district | From whole DAs | From shares of cut DAs | Firmness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Blackfalds | 42.6% (Red Deer-Blackfalds) | 4,423 | 34 | Firm |
| Sylvan Lake | 20.0% (Red Deer-Lacombe) | 2,455 | 749 | Firm |
| Chestermere | 9.3% (Calgary-Peigan-Chestermere) | 387 | 1,675 | Firm: the report states it |
| Bowden | 16.3% (Red Deer-Innisfail) | 0 | 209 | Unresolved |
| Coaldale | 13.7% (Lethbridge-Little Bow) | 0 | 1,199 | Unresolved |
| Okotoks | 9.2% (Calgary-De Winton) | 0 | 2,792 | Unresolved |
| Devon | 5.2% (Stony Plain-Drayton Valley) | 0 | 339 | Unresolved |
| Lac la Biche County | 4.3% (Barrhead-Westlock-Athabasca) | 0 | 331 | Unresolved |

The five unresolved splits need finer population data, such as census dissemination blocks, or a check against current municipal limits.

## Reading

Communities of interest are given as the reason for one split (Chestermere), and highway boundaries for one (Blackfalds). For the other six the report gives no reason for dividing the town. For four of them (Sylvan Lake, Bowden, Okotoks, Devon) its text describes the town as kept whole. Section 14(b) names municipalities themselves as communities of interest, so a split justified on community-of-interest grounds needs another community the commission judged stronger.

Two of those four (Bowden and Okotoks) are among the unresolved splits, so the text and the map may agree once finer population data are used. Sylvan Lake cannot be explained that way: 2,455 of its 2021 residents live in census areas wholly inside Red Deer-Lacombe.
