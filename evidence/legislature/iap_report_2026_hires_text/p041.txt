Edmonton-Windermere

Edmonton-South West (which the 2025-2026 Electoral Boundaries Commission renamed Edmonton-
Windermere in its Final Report) is currently a north-south electoral division between the City's southern
boundary (Highway 19 to Devon) and Anthony Henday Drive. It is bounded on the east by Whitemud
Creek and the Edmonton-South electoral division. Its current (2017) configuration, which we recommend
changing, straddles the North Saskatchewan River on the west. In short, it lies between Devon and the
Edmonton International Airport at its south end and the Anthony Henday Drive at its northern end.
In its Interim Report, the 2025-2026 Electoral Boundaries Commission recommended moving the western
boundary of the electoral division east to the North Saskatchewan River such that the electoral division
would no longer have to straddle the river. The advisory panel concurs in that recommendation which, if
approved, would put residents west of the river in either Edmonton-West Enoch or Edmonton-McLung.
In its Interim Report, the 2025-2026 Electoral Boundaries Commission also adjusted the eastern boundary
of the electoral division somewhat, departing from Whitemud Creek between 28th Avenue and 41st. Avenue
S.W. along Chappelle Link S.W. The neighbourhood of Chappelle Gardens was recommended to be moved
from Edmonton-South into Edmonton-South West (Edmonton-Windermere) to accommodate significant
anticipated growth in Edmonton-South. The advisory panel concurs in that recommendation. But for the
most part the electoral division's eastern boundary is recommended to be Whitemud Creek which is the
current eastern boundary of the electoral division.
The electoral division's population, though lower than the provincial average, is still within variance and rapid
growth is expected. Because the electoral division no longer crosses the North Saskatchewan River or the
Anthony Henday Drive, it may be an easier electoral division for an elected representative to traverse and
represent. It is smaller and arguably more understandable.
The 2025-2026 Electoral Boundaries Commission, in its Final Report, recommended that the name of the
electoral division be changed from Edmonton-South West to Edmonton-Windermere because the cardinal
points of the compass are not as helpful as the name of a defining neighborhood, road or popular golf course
in describing an electoral division. The advisory panel concurs in that recommendation.
