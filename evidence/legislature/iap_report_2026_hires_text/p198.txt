Brent Robinson was born and raised in Okotoks, Alberta, and has lived there with his family since 2011.
Brent earned a BA with Distinction in Economics and Political Science from the University of Calgary in
2001 and an LLB from the University of British Columbia in 2004. He also has a graduate diploma in Data
Science and Analytics from the University of Calgary. He has been a member of the Law Society of Alberta
since 2005. He served as a Councillor for the Town of Okotoks from 2021 to 2025, which included service
on committees of the Calgary Metropolitan Region Board and the Foothills County and Town of Okotoks
Intermunicipal Committee. Brent is a lifelong sports fan who has served on various boards involving amateur
lacrosse. He now spends a large part of his summers travelling to communities across Alberta to help
organize and watch lacrosse as the Executive Director of the Alberta Lacrosse Association.

The Honourable Monte Solberg, P.C., was born in Calgary, Alberta, but grew up in Drumheller, Alberta and
Rosetown, Saskatchewan. He attended the University of Saskatchewan before returning to the family radio
business. He later became the General Manager of Q 13 Radio Brooks and then CHUM Radio Alberta before
becoming the Member of Parliament for Medicine Hat in 1993, a position he held for 15 years. In 2006 Prime
Minister Harper appointed him to serve as Minister of Citizenship and Immigration and then Minister of
Human Resources and Social Development.
During his political career he knocked on doors, attended constituency association meetings and spoke at
nomination meetings in dozens of communities across Alberta. As the longtime Finance critic for his party,
he heard testimony from hundreds of not-for-profit groups, business organizations and individuals from
Alberta during annual Finance Committee hearings.
Upon leaving politics in 2008, he and his wife, Deb, moved to Calgary, where he founded New West Public
Affairs, which serves clients across Canada from operations in Calgary, Edmonton, Toronto and Ottawa.
Monte also sits on the Nature Conservancy of Canada's Alberta Advisory Committee. In addition, he is a
co-chair of Generate Canada. He and his wife split their time between Calgary and the Alberta Badlands
near Drumheller.
