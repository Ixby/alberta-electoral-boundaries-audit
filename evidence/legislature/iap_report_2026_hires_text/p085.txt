Lethbridge

The advisory panel proposes one Lethbridge electoral division that is made up solely of Lethbridge and three
other hybrid electoral divisions made up of Lethbridge residents and residents of towns, villages, and rural
areas of Southern Alberta.
The Lethbridge-only electoral division, if approved, would be comprised of roughly 60,000 people or roughly
60% of Lethbridge's current population.
The proposed electoral division unites the City's downtown with much of West Lethbridge and the eastern
and southeastern communities of Lakeview, Redwood, Fairmont and Southgate (Prairie Arbour Estates). West
of the Oldman River it is roughly comprised of Paradise Canyon, The Canyons, RiverStone, Sunridge, Varsity
Village, West Lethbridge, the University of Lethbridge, West Highlands, Ridgewood and Heritage Heights.
It is bounded on the south by the Oldman River in West Lethbridge and the city limits east of the river. It's
bounded on the west by Metis Trail West. and on the north by Highway 3. On the eastern side the electoral
boundary heads down Mayor Magrath to South Parkside Drive and then east to Highway 4, and then
southeast along the railway tracks and then back up north to take in the Research and Development Centre
and then south along the Lethbridge's eastern city limits.
The recommended electoral division is intended to unite parts of the West Lethbridge with the city's
downtown and mature central neighborhoods. It is a distinct urban electoral division with higher density
and rental housing, downtown commerce, civic and cultural institutions, social health services, transit
dependencies and established (older) neighborhood concerns. A central electoral division is intended to
give residents effective representation of their urban concerns while permitting outer neighborhoods to be
connected for representation purposes to regional corridors to which they are functionally connected.
A more precise description of the boundaries of the proposed electoral division follows:
Commencing at a point on the south boundary of Highway 3 west of the Oldman River at Westside Drive,
then south to Garry Drive and then, westerly to Metis Trail W., southerly to Whoop Up Drive then easterly
to McMaster Blvd and then southerly to Mount Burke Blvd and then westerly at Rocky Mountain Blvd
until Sunridge Blvd and then directly south to the left bank of the Oldman River westerly to the southern
City Limit of Lethbridge to its easterly boundary, then north to South Parkside Drive westerly to Mayor
Magrath Drive then northward to the southerly boundary of Highway 3 and then returning west to the point
of commencement.
