Church Ranches is admittedly right across 12-Mile Coulee Road from the Calgary Bow community of Rocky
Ridge but there are a handful of other Bearspaw subdivisions north of the highway which are also either
adjacent to Rocky Ridge or adjacent to Church Ranches. The issue these communities present is where
do you stop? One might go all the way west to Lochend Road (Highway 766) which could serve as a natural
electoral boundary, but how far north of Church Ranches do you go to possibly take in Rolling Acres, Biggar
Meadows, Biggar Heights and Woodland Estates?
The advisory panel would have preferred more input than its process permitted to be comfortable adding
Church Ranches to the Calgary Bow electoral division. But it is certainly something for the Legislature, or a
future electoral boundaries commission, to consider.
As a result, the independent advisory panel is only recommending the inclusion of the Bearspaw subdivisions
south of Highway 1A. But there is definitely a case to be made for also including Church Ranches.
