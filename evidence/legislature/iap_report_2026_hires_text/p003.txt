Dear Mr. Lunty and Members of the Select Special Committee on Electoral Boundaries:

Pursuant to Government Motion 37 approved by the Legislative Assembly of Alberta,
attached hereto is the report of the independent advisory panel on electoral boundaries for
your review.

It includes the advisory panel's proposals for the areas, boundaries and names of the 91
electoral divisions we were mandated to review.

Also, incorporated in the advisory panel's proposals are the recommendations contained in
Recommendation 5 in the Addendum to the Majority Report of the 2025-2026 Electoral
Boundaries Commission subject to modifications the independent advisory panel
considered necessary as permitted by Government Motion 37. Our proposals to restore the
two electoral divisions the Commission proposed to be removed are contained in our
report.

The Minority Opinion of two of the advisory panel's members is also included in our report.

With the submission of this report, we believe the advisory panel's mandate is
concluded. If the Committee or the Legislative Assembly has questions arising out of any
of our proposals, we are prepared to answer them.

Respectfully submitted this 22nd day of October, 2026.

The Honourable Brian O'Ferrall, Chair

The Honourable Monte Solberg, Panel Member  Darwin K. Durnie, Panel Member
