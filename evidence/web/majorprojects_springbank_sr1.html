Springbank Off-Stream Dry Reservoir (SR1)

1915
Jan 0001
This is an approved Government of Alberta capital project. 


The Springbank Off-Stream Reservoir is a 3,700 acre dry reservoir that will store water temporarily during a flood. It will work in tandem with the Glenmore Reservoir in Calgary. Together, the combined storage capacity would accommodate water volumes equal to the 2013 flood.
 
The project supported 2,200 jobs during construction.
