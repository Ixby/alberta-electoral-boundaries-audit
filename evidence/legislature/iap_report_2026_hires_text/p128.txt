d. Making the electoral divisions in Edmonton more coherent.

32. Creation of this electoral division does not unduly dilute urban representation. Indeed, it enhances
     Edmonton representation to a significant extent. There is also no other reasonable way to achieve these
     objectives--unless the map of the entire province is to be adjusted. Only 8 non-Edmonton electoral
     divisions are affected: Fort McMurray-Wood Buffalo, Fort McMurray-Lac La Biche, St. Albert-Sturgeon
     (which is renamed Morinville-Sturgeon), Barrhead-Westlock-Athabasca, Stony Plain-Drayton Valley
     (which becomes Drayton Valley-Devon), Spruce Grove, Leduc-Devon (which becomes Leduc-
     Beaumont), and West Yellowhead. This minimizes cascading impacts in accordance with Justice Miller's
     conditions.

B) Rocky Mountain House-Sundre

33. The Panel unanimously proposes the second restored electoral division be "Rocky Mountain House-
     Sundre", consisting mostly of:

a) The vast majority of Clearwater County;
b) The most western parts of Mountain View County including all of Sundre and Cremona and

     surrounding areas;
c) Portions of the MD of Bighorn presently in Rimbey-Rocky Mountain House-Sundre; and
d) Portions of the MD of Bighorn and Banff National Park north of the Bow River watershed.

34. This has impacts on only six other electoral divisions:

a) Mountain View-Kneehill (renamed Olds-Didsbury-Three Hills): loses the most western portions of
     Mountain View County, including Sundre and Cremona, so as to concentrate on its agricultural core
     in the Highway 2 corridor;

b) Lacombe-Clearwater (renamed Lacombe-Sylvan Lake): becomes all of the areas surrounding Sylvan
     Lake, most of Lacombe County, the City of Lacombe, the southeastern portions of Ponoka County,
     a small piece of Clearwater County, and the portions of Ponoka County immediately north of Gull
     Lake;

c) Wetaskiwin-Ponoka-Maskwacis (renamed Wetaskiwin-Ponoka): becomes all of Wetaskiwin County,
     and almost all of Ponoka County, save for discrete portions included in Lacombe-Sylvan Lake given
     the need for population balance and the desire to keep all Gull Lake communities together;

d) Drumheller-Stettler: loses portions of Lacombe County, which were an odd fit in this sprawling
     eastern electoral division;

e) Sylvan Lake-Innisfail (renamed Blackfalds-Innisfail): becomes most of Red Deer County, except the
     areas including and immediately around Sylvan Lake, while also including the areas immediately
     surrounding and including Blackfalds; and

f) Canmore-Banff: loses portions presently in Rimbey-Rocky Mountain House-Sundre and north of the
     Bow River watershed, which share more in common with the Eastern Slopes from transportation,
     cultural, and economic perspectives.
