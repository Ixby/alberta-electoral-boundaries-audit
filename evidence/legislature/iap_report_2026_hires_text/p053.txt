Lacombe-Sylvan Lake

With the restoration of a Rocky Mountain House electoral division (Rocky Mountain House-Sundre), the
advisory panel was able to recommend an electoral division that put Lacombe back into an electoral division
focused on the Highway 2 Corridor rather than on the West Country. The recommended removal of Rocky
Mountain House-Sundre had meant that Ponoka was put in a huge Clearwater County/Mountain View
County electoral division. This no longer needs to happen.
The proposed electoral division would include Gull Lake and Bentley to the west and a portion of Buffalo
Lake to the east as well as Sylvan Lake to the southwest.
Because the Lacombe-Sylvan Lake electoral division extends all the way to Buffalo Lake, there would have
to be changes to the west end of the electoral division of Drumheller-Stettler as mapped by the 2025-2026
Electoral Boundaries Commission. The Commission had mapped the western boundary of Drumheller-
Stettler extending all the way up to the town limits of Lacombe and Morningside which put a large riding
east of the Red Deer River (Drumheller-Stettler) adjacent to Highway 2. That was, perhaps, unavoidable but
nevertheless incongruous.
