Blackfalds-Innisfail

The advisory panel's proposed Blackfalds-Innisfail electoral division is an electoral division that surrounds
Red Deer on all sides. It includes Blackfalds with a population of roughly 12,000 people and Innisfail with a
population of about 8,000 people. It takes in a bit of Red Deer at the north end and the Westerner Park, Red
Deer's fairgrounds, hockey and rodeo arenas, racetrack and exhibition hall and several other agriculture-
related service centres at the south end.
South and west of Red Deer the Blackfalds-Innisfail electoral division includes Penhold, Innisfail, Spruce
View, the Dickson Dam and Gleniffer Lake. It does not include Sylvan Lake which the advisory panel
recommends for the Lacombe-Sylvan Lake electoral division. Nor does it include Bowden which we
recommend for Olds-Didsbury-Three Hills. South and east, it includes Delburne, Elnora and Pine Lake.
