## Relevance

§5.8.4 of `report_academic.md` documents the minority map's pairing of Enoch Cree Nation with Devon, a town 50 km to the south on Highway 60. This data confirms the COI critique: Enoch Cree residents commute north toward Edmonton, not south toward Devon. The pairing is not supported by commuter-flow community-of-interest evidence.

Whether the Devon bundling is population-math-constrained (no compliant alternative) has not been separately verified. The commuter-flow finding is the documented fact; the motive/necessity question is flagged open.
