# Trust-nothing sweep and its fixes, 2026-11-09

Read-only sweep of the audit corpus at `557d11fe`, run 2026-11-09 (Alberta time). 15 specialist reviewers each checked one slice; an adversarial verifier re-checked every finding; a completeness critic listed what no slice covered. **288 confirmed, 13 refuted, 4 uncertain.** Seven items sent beforehand were verified in the same run (6 confirmed, 1 refuted).

Fixes followed the author's rule, "triple check before altering the record":
1. A fixer re-checked each confirmed finding against its primary source (the third check) and applied it, held it, or proposed wording.
2. An independent reviewer then checked every applied change against its source (the fourth check). Six reviews covered 419 changed passages. They marked 22 for correction and none for reversion, and added notes on others. The corrections, and the notes that fixed a fact, were applied before commit; so were related lines the reviewers found outside the changes (X4 and X5 wording, change notes, cross-surface mismatches).
3. The corrected changes were merged and committed in `4bb2f6f3`. That commit message says 256; the count below is the exact one.

**Outcome for the 295 verified items (288 findings + 7 sent items):** 258 applied, 18 held for the author, 18 left as judgment calls with proposed wording, 1 rejected on re-check.

No file under `preregistration/`, the signed amendment log, the Author's View, or OSF was changed. Reader-facing text carries no change notes; git is the history.

## Cross-cutting corrections

- **"1 in 568,000"** is a model estimate (the parametric χ² tail, doubled for two channels), not a bound or ceiling. The n_eff-adjusted (Hotelling) figure, doubled, is about 1 in 450,000 (`findings/joint_outlier_score.json`). Written "p ≈", not "p ≤", outside the author's signed text.
- **The "ESS-adjusted lower bound ≈p98"** for seats at 50/50 is dropped everywhere: no script computes it (it entered as prose in `6ac2573e`).
- **The November tests** were written in June 2026 and amended Nov 2–7 (Amendments 7–13); 7–10 before any of the panel's geometry was scored, 11–13 after results were seen. The reconstruction failed its first two calibrations (49.0%, 13.8% against a 10% limit); Amendment 10 Part 1 withdrew the one-attempt limit; the third attempt passed as a reproducibility run on pages already seen. Amendment 12 is cited with its own "Not blind" qualifier.
- **Finding 9's compactness test** could not be run on the canonical data (no plan reaches the minority's value); 0.239/0.234 came from a superseded ensemble.
- **South-central redraws** keep every district within ±25% of the region's mean with no s.15(2) branch, so they cannot draw a Canmore-Banff; the "outlier" result is qualified beside each statement of it. Lethbridge-West alone stays past p95.
- **Lalande (2026 SCC 13)** restated from the archived judgment.
- **Missing ballots** are spread by each voting area's election-day vote count, not its lean.
- **Seeds.** The anchoring seed (80,780,579) and the Section C seed (3,562,959,107) both reproduce under the derivation formula OSF s58a6 registers; the "cannot be reproduced" notes were wrong (they used `drand_seed.py`'s round-5,500,000 formula).
