Alberta population estimates

A population estimate is a measure of the number of people residing in an area at a particular point in time. 

Highlights

These are the key highlights for the second quarter of 2026:

  • Among the four largest provinces, Alberta recorded the strongest population growth in the second quarter of 2026.
  • Net outflows of non-permanent residents (NPRs) continued to dampen international migration, although losses were smaller than in the first quarter.
  • Alberta continued to lead the country in interprovincial migration, recording the strongest net inflow among all provinces.

For more information and analysis, read the Second Quarter Population Report.

Alberta population projections

Projections give a picture of future population based on trends. Visit the Alberta Population Projections Dashboard for the latest long-term interactive projection. 

Highlights

  • Alberta’s population is projected to reach 7 million adding roughly 1.9 million residents over the next 26 years between 2025 and 2051: an average annual growth rate of 1.3%.
  • The majority of the expected growth (81%) will be due to migration, with 62% coming from international migration and 19% from interprovincial migration. Natural increase is also projected to contribute 19%.
