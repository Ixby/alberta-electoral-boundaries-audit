Introduction

The Office of Statistics and Information (OSI) is the official Alberta statistical agency, responsible for the collection, analysis and dissemination of data and the development and publication of statistics for the province of Alberta.

As the provincial focal point for Statistics Canada, the OSI represents Alberta’s interests and collaborates with its federal, provincial and territorial partners on national statistical issues.

Statistics by topic

Demographic statistics
Data and analysis on Alberta’s population, such as current population estimates, population projections and components of growth.
Economic statistics

Various statistics and analysis, including economic multipliers and spatial price surveys.

Business, industry and trade statistics
Data, reports and other information regarding Alberta's industries, business investment, exports and primary industries.
Education statistics
Selected reports, fact sheets and resources on Alberta’s K to 12 and post-secondary education systems.
Environment statistics
Data and analysis on Alberta’s environmental sector, such as information on air quality, emissions, water and climate.
Geospatial data and statistics

Learn more about statistical and administrative boundaries used in provision of census geographies and analysis.

Government statistics
Government of Alberta, Government of Canada and Alberta municipality data and statistical information.
Health statistics
Statistics and analysis on Alberta’s health sector, such as health data and dashboards.
Housing statistics
Data and analysis on housing in Alberta.
Labour statistics
Employment, wage and salary information, including job market forecasts and trends produced by Alberta departments.
Social statistics

Selected databases, dashboards, reports, fact sheets and resources about societies and communities in Alberta.

  • Additional Canadian resources

    • Statistics Canada – Ensures Canadians have the key information on Canada's economy, society and environment that they require to function effectively as citizens and decision makers.
    • Canadian Association of Petroleum Producers – Features oil and natural gas industry publications and statistics.
    • Canadian Research Data Centre Network – Provides university, government and other approved researchers with social, economic and health confidential microdata in secure computer facilities located on university campuses across Canada.
    • Signal49 – Reports on organizational performance, public policy, economic data and trends (formerly The Conference Board of Canada).

  • Additional international resources

    • International Energy Agency – Includes data and reports on the global energy market.
    • Organization for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) data – Features reports and data on OECD countries.
    • U.S. Census Bureau – Provides data about the U.S. economy and its people.
    • U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics – Includes data tools, publications and forecasts.
    • The World Factbook (United States Central Intelligence Agency)
    • United Nations Statistics Division – Provides global statistical information, develops standards and norms for statistical activities, and supports countries' efforts to strengthen their national statistical systems.
    • United Nations Economic Commission for Europe – Provides free access to data structured to facilitate easy retrieval of statistics, organized by subject or policy areas.
    • World of Statistics – Features the world population clock.
