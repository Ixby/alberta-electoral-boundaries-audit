1. Acknowledgements

The Independent Advisory Panel acknowledges those who have contributed over many decades to the
development of Alberta's electoral map and, in particular, to the members of the 2025-2026 Alberta
Electoral Boundaries Commission. Its commissioners did much of the heavy lifting in recommending the
boundaries of most of the Province's electoral divisions. Electoral boundary mapping is an iterative process,
and each boundary commission or review panel is assisted by its predecessors. Our panel, of course, was
also assisted by the Legislature's decision to add two more electoral divisions which allowed us to restore
the two electoral divisions the Commission recommended the Legislature remove.
The advisory panel was also assisted by the recommendations of the much-maligned Minority
Commissioners. They gave the advisory panel much to consider in terms of whether effective representation
involves a reconciliation of diverse interests or advocating for a specific community of interests.
Electoral boundary determinations are as important as they are difficult and complex; and the work of all
who have participated in the process is to be valued, not to be deprecated.
