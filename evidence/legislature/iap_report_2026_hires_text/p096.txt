Banff, Canmore, Kananaskis, Longview, Diamond Valley, Millarville, Priddis and Bragg Creek form a logically
connected mountain-foothills community of interest. They share tourism, and conservation pressures,
housing affordability challenges, highway access issues, emergency response demands, watershed and
park management concerns.
These communities are not merely adjacent, they are connected by common geography, weather,
infrastructure, and common provincial policy concerns. They all experience intense visitor and seasonal
traffic. They must balance development with watershed protection and wildfire risk, and they face having a
labour-market pressure tied to tourism, outdoor recreation and proximity to Calgary. These are not generic
rural issues or generic urban issues. These are recognizable mountain-foothills playground issues.
Finally, separating Diamond Valley from Okotoks gives Okotoks its own representative to which it will soon be
entitled if it continues to grow the way it has.
The electoral division continues to include the Stoney First Nation at Morley and the Ghost River
communities of Waiparous, Beaupre and Benchlands, as well as the Stoney's Rabbit Hill First Nation
near Benchlands. Portions of the M.D. of Bighorn No. 8 and Rocky View County are also included in the
northeastern part of the electoral division.
