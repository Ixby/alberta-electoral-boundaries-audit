Having read the law governing electoral boundary making, which in many ways is in its infancy in this
country, my opinion is that the proposals contained in this report are not unconstitutional. They do not
offend the Charter. They do not constitute a collateral attack on the 2025-2026 Electoral Boundaries
Commission recommendations. The principle does not apply to non-binding recommendations. Nor do
the panel's proposals violate administrative law principles of procedural fairness. The panel's proposals
are not unprecedented and they do not exceed the panel's mandate. It is the minority's 40-page polemic
passing judgment on the constitutionality of the panel's process and its proposals which exceeds the
panel's mandate.
The panel's proposals deserve to be assessed only on the basis of their merits or otherwise, applying the
Electoral Boundaries Commission Act and the principles the case law prescribes for recommending the
boundaries of proposed electoral divisions.
