Morinville-Sturgeon

As a consequence of the recommended restoration of the Lac Ste. Anne electoral division, the advisory
panel recommends that the current (2017) boundaries of the electoral division known as Morinville-St. Albert
be substantially restored, except that eastern portions of the current (2017) Lac Ste. Anne-Parkland would be
added to it. The panel would also recommend re-naming the electoral division Morinville-Sturgeon because
it would be comprised of Morinville and all of Sturgeon County except for the Alexander First Nation Reserve.
As St. Albert is already the name of an electoral division comprised of most, but not all, of St. Albert, the
name may help avoiding confusion.
The 2025-2026 Electoral Boundaries Commission had reluctantly recommended increasing the size of the
current (2017) Morinville-St. Albert electoral division which the Commission recommended renaming St.
Albert-Sturgeon. Notwithstanding the restoration of the Lac Ste. Anne electoral division, the panel proposes
to push the boundary of the Morinville-Sturgeon electoral division west to parallel Highway 779, except that
the Alexander First Nation Reserve would remain in the Lac Ste. Anne electoral division, but Pine Sands on
Sandy Lake would become part of Morinville-Sturgeon.
The Morinville-Sturgeon electoral division that the panel recommends has clear and understandable
boundaries. Its southern boundary is the City of Edmonton and the southern border of Sturgeon County.
Its eastern boundary is the North Saskatchewan River. Its western boundary is the western boundary
of Sturgeon County. And the electoral division's northern boundary is roughly the northern boundary of
Sturgeon County.
The electoral division includes the airbase at Namao, the northeast quadrant of the City of St. Albert and
the Town of Morinville, further north along Highway 2. It also includes Legal, Bon Accord, Gibbons, and
Red Water.
In the panel's view, it might be preferable to call this electoral division Morinville-Sturgeon (as opposed to
St. Albert-Sturgeon) because it takes in the entire town of Morinville, and only a part of St. Albert. It also
avoids the confusion of having St. Albert in the name of two electoral divisions. The electoral division
is also comprised of most of Sturgeon County. Sturgeon County administers the western half of the
electoral division.
The 2025-2026 Electoral Boundaries Commission recommended that the border between the St. Albert
electoral division and what we are now calling the Morinville-Sturgeon electoral division be moved within the
City of St. Albert such that all areas south of the Sturgeon River in St. Albert would be mapped in the City of
St. Albert electoral division. We concur in that recommendation
The relatively low population of Morinville-Sturgeon at 47,131 or 12% below average may soon be
ameliorated. There is extraordinary growth in Sturgeon County's east end which should only increase with
Meta's $13-billion artificial intelligence data centre proposed to be built in Sturgeon Country within Alberta's
Industrial Heartland near Fort Saskatchewan.
