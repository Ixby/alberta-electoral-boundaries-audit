# IAP districts unchanged from the commission majority map (Step A)

Amendment 7, Step A. This compares two tables and scores nothing.

A district counts as unchanged when its name **and** its stated population exactly match a district in the 2025–26 Electoral Boundaries Commission's majority map. Names are compared after removing the commission list's trailing asterisks and dashes and straightening curly apostrophes.

Sources:

- **IAP:** *2026 Independent Advisory Panel on Electoral Boundaries report*, Appendix 7, "Electoral Division Populations", printed pp.205–206 (PDF 211–212). The figures are transcribed in the audit's session data and verified against the report's text layer: hi-res PDF, SHA-256 `d8ae78ab…75122`; low-res copy in `evidence/legislature/iap_report_2026_lowres.pdf`.

- **Commission:** 2025–26 Alberta Electoral Boundaries Commission final report (`evidence/commission_reports/abebc_2026_rpt_final.pdf`), majority list of new electoral divisions, with population, by ED number (PDF pp.46–56).

- **Check:** the 89 commission populations sum to 4,888,723, the same base and total as the IAP's 91.


**Result:** 56 of 91 IAP districts are unchanged; 35 are changed. Two changed districts differ by only 2 people each (Calgary-Acadia, Red Deer-South). Under the exact rule they count as changed, and are flagged.
