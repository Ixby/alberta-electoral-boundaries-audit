As far as naming the Lac Ste. Anne electoral division is concerned, we would simply recommend
"Lac Ste. Anne".
The restoration of the Lac Ste. Anne electoral division has, of course, impacted adjacent electoral divisions
as mapped by the 2025-2026 Electoral Boundaries Commission. Some of the impacted electoral divisions
can be restored to their current boundaries. Others need to be modified if Lac Ste. Anne is to be restored.
And those changes, in turn, affect other electoral divisions remote from Lac Ste. Anne because of what
are referred to as "cascading" effects. Changes to the boundaries of one electoral division or the insertion
of a "new" electoral division in already mapped territory requires changes to the boundaries of electoral
divisions elsewhere.
Electoral divisions, as mapped by the 2025-2026 Electoral Boundaries commission, that would have to be
modified if the proposed restoration of Lac Ste. Anne is approved will be dealt with separately in this report,
but they include:

   1) To the north: Barrhead-Westlock-Athabasca;
   2) To the east: Morinville-St. Albert (formerly St. Albert-Sturgeon);
   3) To the west: West Yellowhead;
   4) To the south: Spruce Grove-Stony Plain and Drayton Valley-Devon; and
   5) As far away as Fort McMurray-Lac La Biche, adjustments had to be made.
In conclusion, the advisory panel proposes an electoral division called "Lac Ste. Anne" roughly consisting of:
   1) The Town of Stony Plain;
   2) All of Lac Ste. Anne County;
   3) Lake Wabamun
   4) The most northerly portions of Parkland County as shown on the accompanying map.
This is a coherent electoral division west and north of Edmonton. There is a primary highway running through
it, Highway 43, which connects its communities. The electoral division has a reasonable population. And the
recommended boundaries of the electoral division appear to provide its constituents with the opportunity
to obtain effective representation which the Legislature's resolution quite properly directed the panel to take
into consideration.
