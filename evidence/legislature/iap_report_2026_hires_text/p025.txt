West Yellowhead

West Yellowhead is a huge electoral division including all of Jasper National Park and Willmore Wilderness
Park. It also includes Whitecourt, Edson, Obed, Hinton, Grande Cache, Jasper, Cadomin, Robb and
Coal Valley.
This is yet another electoral division which the 2025-2026 Electoral Boundaries Commission "reluctantly"
had to change as a result of the removal of Lac Ste. Anne-Parkland, although the changes were not drastic.
In any event, the advisory panel is able to recommend its restoration. That restoration involved moving
Mayerthorpe out of West Yellowhead, as mapped by the 2025-2026 Electoral Boundaries Commission, back
into Lac Ste. Anne where it was situated in the last general election.
