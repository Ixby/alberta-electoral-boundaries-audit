Barrhead-Westlock-Athabasca

This electoral division, as mapped by the 2025-2026 Electoral Boundaries Commission, was one of the
electoral divisions impacted by the elimination of the Lac Ste. Anne -Parkland electoral division. However,
now, with the restoration of the Lac Ste. Anne electoral division, we are proposing boundaries for Barrhead-
Westlock-Athabasca which are roughly the same as the current Athabasca-Barrhead-Westlock electoral
division boundaries. The status quo is maintained and in the absence of a pressing need to make changes,
maintaining the status quo is considered good practice in electoral boundary mapping. Studies have
found that maintaining the status quo in clear and understandable electoral divisions promotes collective
recognition of community concerns over time. It has also been found to promote recognition of elected
representatives by constituents who all too often do not know who their elected representative is.

When Lac Ste. Anne-Parkland was recommended for removal, portions of it, including Lac Ste. Anne itself,
was put into Barrhead-Westlock-Athabasca. But when the Legislature indicated a desire to have the Lac
Ste. Anne-Parkland electoral division restored, the advisory panel found that it could be restored, but the
restoration removed a significant portion of Barrhead-Westlock-Athabasca as mapped in the 2026-2026
Electoral Boundaries Commission's Final Report. And so, the advisory panel is proposing an electoral division
which is similar to the current (2017) Athabasca-Barrhead-Westlock electoral division with two changes.

The first change is that Swan Hills and Fort Assiniboine are not recommended to be included in the
Barrhead-Westlock-Athabasca electoral division. The 2025-2026 Electoral Boundaries Commission
recommended putting Swan Hills and Fort Assiniboine in the Lesser Slave Lake electoral division. The
reason was because the population of the Lesser Slave Lake electoral division had fallen below 50% of the
average population of all electoral divisions in the Province. Recommending such an electoral division is not
permitted by the Electoral Boundaries Commission Act. The Commission's reasoning therefore struck us as
being reasonable.

Because of the expansion of the Legislature to 91 electoral divisions and the resulting drop in the average
population of the Province's electoral divisions, the current boundaries of Lesser Slave Lake make it eligible
for Section 15(2) status without additional territory being added. Accordingly, a case could be made that
Lesser Slave Lake should revert to its current boundaries to preserve the status quo and increase the
percentage of the population that is indigenous. We are nonetheless recommending keeping those areas
in Lesser Slave Lake rather than the proposed Barrhead-Westlock-Athabasca for three reasons. First,
the MLA for the current Athabasca-Barhead-Westlock expressed concerns about representing too many
municipalities. Removing Swan Hills and Fort Assiniboine removes all portions of Big Lakes County and
Woodlands County from his electoral division. Secondly, the rate of population growth in Lesser Slave Lake
is very low indeed. Thirdly, it was suggested in Recommendation 5, that the Commission's majority map be
maintained to the extent possible. Keeping its map of Lesser Slave Lake helps in that respect.

A second change to the current Athabasca-Barrhead-Westlock electoral division the panel recommends
is to add the Saddle Lake Cree Nation's Reserve at the southeastern extremity of the electoral division.
The Saddle Lake Cree Nation's Reserve is currently mapped in the Bonnyville-Cold Lake-St. Paul electoral
division. The Saddle Lake Cree Nation claims roughly 11,000 members. Portions of the County of St. Paul
were recommended by the 2025-2026 Electoral Boundaries Commission to be moved to Bonnyville-Cold
Lake-St. Paul because it unites most of the County of St. Paul in the same electoral division. We concur in
that recommendation.

We would propose to name this slightly modified electoral division "Barrhead-Westlock-Athabasca"
electoral division. Like the current, Athabasca-Barrhead electoral division, it still contains Barrhead,
Westlock and Athabasca, as well as Smoky Lake County, Boyle and Vilna. The 2025-2026 Electoral
Boundaries Commission had recommended moving Smoky Lake County to Fort McMurray-Athabasca
for reasons which no longer obtain and therefore the advisory panel is recommending it be put back into
Barrhead-Westlock-Athabasca.
