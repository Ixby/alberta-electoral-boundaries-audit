2. Executive Summary

On June 9, 2026, the independent advisory panel (which will sometimes be referred to as the advisory
panel or merely the panel) was appointed by the Select Special Committee of the Legislature on Electoral
Boundaries pursuant to Government Motion 37 approved by the Legislative Assembly April 21, 2026.

The mandate of the independent advisory panel is dealt with in greater detail elsewhere in this report, but for
the purposes of this executive summary the panel's mandate was to divide the Province into 91 geographic
electoral divisions and then review and make proposals with respect to those electoral divisions. In addition,
the panel was mandated to incorporate in its proposals certain recommendations made by the Chairman of
the 2025-2026 Electoral Boundaries Commission.

I am pleased to report that we discharged our mandate as completely as could have been expected in the
time the panel was given. I am also pleased to say that while not all members of the advisory panel agree
with all the proposals or recommendations which are summarized below, all members cooperated and
participated in the process.

That said, far too much of the panel's time and energy was expended dealing with mandate and
constitutional issues raised by the minority members of the panel. I deal with those issues later in the report.

The advisory panel reviewed all of the Province's electoral divisions from several perspectives. We reviewed
the current 87 electoral divisions. We reviewed the 89 electoral divisions proposed by the 2025-2026
Electoral Boundary Commission. And, in accordance with our mandate the advisory panel reviewed and
recommended the restoration of the two electoral divisions the Commission had removed, but with slightly
different names: Lac Ste. Anne and Rocky Mountain House-Sundre.

Restoring the Lac Ste. Anne electoral division compelled us in some cases and prompted us in other cases
to make proposals for electoral divisions impacted or downstream of the restored Lac Ste. Anne electoral
division. Those included Spruce Grove, Morinville-St. Albert (which we propose to rename Morinville-
Sturgeon), Barrhead-Westlock-Athabasca, Fort McMurry-Lac La Biche, West Yellowhead and Drayton
Valley-Devon. Maps and narratives describing these proposals are to be found in the pages that follow.

In restoring the Rocky Mountain House-Sundre electoral division, the advisory panel was also compelled
or prompted to make proposals which would change Highway 2 corridor electoral divisions somewhat,
including recommending changes to Maskwacis-Wetaskiwin, (which we propose to change to Wetaskiwin-
Ponoka), Lacombe-Ponoka (which we propose to change to Lacombe-Sylvan Lake), Innisfail-Sylvan Lake
(which we propose to change to Blackfalds-Innisfail), Olds-Didsbury-Three Hills (which we propose to extend
to the southeast) and Drumheller-Stettler (which we propose to return to its pre-Commission boundaries).

Restoring the Rocky Mountain House electoral division also required the panel to consider changes to Banff-
Kananaskis. In its stead, the panel proposes a new Banff-Canmore-Diamond Valley electoral division which,
if approved, would obviate the necessity of making it a Section 15(2) special consideration electoral division.

The advisory panel is also recommending the restoration of a Leduc-Beaumont electoral division, an idea
which the Chairman of the Electoral Boundaries Commission suggested might be studied. The panel studied
it, liked it and is recommending it. However, in order for that recommendation to work, the panel is proposing
the creation of an Edmonton-Nisku electoral division to replace the Edmonton-Beaumont electoral division
which the Commission had proposed. The creation of an Edmonton-Nisku electoral division impacted
Edmonton-South, which in turn required changes to Edmonton-Ellerslie and Edmonton-Meadows. In the
process, the panel was able to recommend changes to Edmonton electoral divisions south of the North
Saskatchewan River that our Edmonton panel member suggested were desirable and were part of the 2025-
2026 Commission Chairman's Recommendation 5. Those recommended changes were to Edmonton-South,
Edmonton-Windermere (formerly Edmonton-South West), Edmonton-Whitemud and Edmonton-Strathcona.
